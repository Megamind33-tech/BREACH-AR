About Viro WorkCare and Orange Mobility Solutions

We build and run software that keeps an organization’s computers healthy, secure and supported.

What we do

Viro WorkCare looks after the Windows computers in an organization. It watches their health, keeps them updated, fixes common problems on its own, and tells the people responsible what to repair, upgrade or replace, with the reasons.

It is made for organizations that depend on their computers but may not have a full IT department: schools, offices and similar places.

How we work

  • Say what we did, and why. Every recommendation comes with its reasons, and every repair is checked afterwards.
  • Ask before we act on someone’s computer. Remote help is announced to the person at the computer, and logged in the audit history.
  • Hide nothing. Every administrative action is in the audit log.
