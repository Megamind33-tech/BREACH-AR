What you get with Viro Care

Fix it, then prove it

One click clears temporary files, gives back idle memory and turns off start-up programs that only slow you down. Viro measures your PC before and after, so you see what actually changed.

  • Before and after, measured on your PC, not estimated
  • Every fix has an Undo, and Viro tells you if one did not work
  • Programs Windows depends on are never touched

Know what is wearing out, and what it will cost

Viro reads your drives, battery, memory and cooling, and says in plain words what to do. It weighs repair against replacement for a PC of your age, with an estimated price.

  • Warnings for failing drives and tired batteries before they fail
  • Repair, upgrade or replace, with the reasons and a price
  • How the PC has been used: Windows upgrades, drive hours, unsafe shut-downs

Get a new PC without starting over

Back up your files, wallpaper and settings, bookmarks, Wi-Fi networks and your list of programs. On the new PC, sign in, enter your passphrase and put it all back.

  • Encrypted on your PC before it leaves it: Viro cannot read it
  • Programs are reinstalled for you; the ones Windows cannot find are listed
  • Nothing on the new PC is overwritten

Buying or selling a used PC? Show the proof

Viro inspects the PC afresh and emails a signed certificate straight to the buyer. The seller cannot edit it. It says how old the PC is, what is worn, what repairs to expect and what it is worth.

  • Sent only to the buyer, so it cannot be faked
  • The buyer checks the serial number on the certificate page
  • Honest about what it could not see

Questions

Which computers does it work on?

Windows 10 and Windows 11 PCs. Viro is built for Windows only today: it does not run on Mac, Linux or phones. It also tells you when a PC cannot run Windows 11 and what that means for its security updates.

Is it safe to let Viro change my PC?

Viro checks the result of every change and keeps what it needs to undo it. It refuses to touch Windows itself, Microsoft runtimes and Viro. A program removed by force is moved aside, not deleted, so it can be brought back.

What does the free plan include?

A full scan that tells you what is wrong, clearing temporary files, start-up programs, memory, the installed programs list with sizes, and Windows and program updates. It does not expire.

How do I pay?

By mobile money, bank transfer or cash. You place an order, pay quoting your reference, and tell us you have paid. Your plan starts as soon as we confirm the money has arrived.

What if I lose my backup passphrase?

Viro cannot recover it, because we never have it. That is what keeps your files private. Write it down somewhere safe when you create a backup.

Who is behind Viro?

Orange Mobility Solutions, a company registered in Zambia and based in Ndola. You can write or call us any time.
