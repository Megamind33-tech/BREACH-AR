# Viro WorkCare: marketing and ads blueprint (2026-10-04)

Goal: turn the free tools into paying customers (Viro Care K 250 a year, Care + Help K 450 a year) and the shop and school plans into demonstrations, using the workflow that already exists. Nothing here needs a new product; three small pieces of plumbing are listed at the end.

## 1. Who we sell to (in this order)

| Segment | Why they pay | Offer | Where they are |
|---|---|---|---|
| Everyday PC owners with a slow or old Windows PC | They would otherwise pay a shop K 100+ for a clean-up, or buy a new PC | Free scan, then Viro Care (fix with before/after, weekly care, backup check) | Facebook, WhatsApp groups, TikTok |
| People replacing a PC | Fear of losing files, wallpaper, programs | **Viro Move**: sign in on the new PC and everything comes back | Facebook, WhatsApp, Google search "transfer files to new laptop" |
| Computer shops and technicians | A signed certificate raises the price of a used PC and builds trust | Shop plan (certificates) | WhatsApp, in person, shop-to-shop referral |
| Schools, offices, NGOs | One screen for every PC, alerts, automatic fixes | Demonstration request (organization console) | Direct visits, LinkedIn, email, referrals |

**The whole-PC pillar (lead with it in every channel).** Viro is not only a software cleaner. It reads the hardware (drives and their wear, battery, cooling, memory, board, graphics, screen), estimates the real age, prices a comparable used and new PC, and answers repair, upgrade or replace with the cost. This is what free cleaners cannot do and what people pay a technician for. It speaks to three buyers at once: owners ("is my PC worth fixing?"), people buying a used PC ("is this one sound and fairly priced?"), and technicians and sellers (a signed report to show). It is now a full section on the landing page.

The message for the first two is one line: **"Slow PC? Fix it, prove it, and never lose your files when you change PCs."** Say what happened ("Freed 14 GB, startup 41 s to 19 s"), never "optimize".

## 3. Channels and what to run

Zambia is mobile-first and WhatsApp-first, so the plan is organic and WhatsApp led, with small paid tests.

1. **WhatsApp (main channel, free).** A WhatsApp Business profile with the catalog set to the two plans, a click-to-chat button on the landing page, and a short broadcast message. This is also where "I have paid" screenshots arrive, so it doubles as support. Use +260 976 092 585 only if you are happy for it to be the public support line.
2. **Facebook and Instagram ads (paid test).** Budget K 500 to K 1,000 for two weeks, targeting Lusaka, Ndola and Kitwe, 20 to 55, interested in laptops and computers. Two ad sets: *slow PC* and *new PC, keep everything*. Send to the landing page, optimise for link clicks first, then for downloads once tracking exists.
3. **TikTok and Reels (organic).** 20 to 30 second screen recordings: the app reading the hardware ("battery 86%, cooling needs service, worth K ..."), the app scanning, "freed 14 GB", before/after startup time, then Viro Move restoring a wallpaper on a clean PC. No voice needed; captions are enough.
4. **Google (later).** Search ads on "slow laptop fix Zambia", "transfer files new laptop". Start only when the site records downloads, otherwise the spend cannot be judged.
5. **Shops and technicians (relationship channel).** Give three shops a free month of the shop plan in exchange for putting the certificate with every refurbished PC they sell. Each certificate carries the Viro name and a QR code that opens the verify page, so every sale advertises us.
6. **Schools and offices.** A one-page proposal, a 30-minute demonstration on the demo organization, and a 30-day pilot on five PCs. The console and the monthly report are the proof.

## 4. Ad copy to start with

- **Slow PC:** "Your PC is slow because of leftovers, not because it is old. Viro finds them, fixes them, and shows you the before and after. Free to scan." Button: *Download free*.
- **New PC:** "Changing laptops? Back up everything to your Viro account: files, programs, wallpaper, Wi-Fi. Sign in on the new PC and it is all there." Button: *See Viro Move*.
- **Worth it?:** "Is your PC worth repairing or time to replace? Viro reads every part, tells you its real age and what it is worth, and gives one clear answer with the cost." Button: *Check my PC free*.
- **Buying used:** "Before you pay for a used laptop, check the real battery, drive and age. Ask the seller for a Viro certificate." Button: *Learn more*.
- **Shops:** "Sell used PCs with a signed Viro certificate: real health, real age, real value. Buyers trust what they can verify." Button: *Ask for a demonstration*.
- **Proof line for every ad:** "Pay by Airtel Money, MTN, bank or cash. No card needed."

## 5. Prices and offers

- Keep K 250 and K 450 a year for now; the comparison section already positions the paid plan against the free cleaners.
- Launch offer worth testing: first 100 buyers get Viro Move storage doubled (costs little). Do not discount the price itself in the first month; you need to learn what people will pay.
- Referral: a buyer who brings a friend gets two months added. Needs the small build in section 7.

## 6. What to measure (weekly, one page)

Visits to the landing page, downloads, scans completed in the app, "Fix my PC" runs, upgrade-card taps, checkouts started, orders placed, orders paid, payment confirmation time, weekly care active PCs, renewals. The two numbers that decide everything: **download-to-paid conversion** and **cost per paid customer**. If a K 1,000 test brings fewer than 4 paid customers, change the message before spending more.

## 7. Plumbing to build (small, in this order)

1. **Source tracking.** Add `?utm_source=...&utm_campaign=...` to every link, store them on the order, and show the source in Billing. Without this the ad spend cannot be judged. (Small: one column and a few lines in checkout.)
2. **Privacy-friendly counters.** Count landing visits, downloads and checkout starts on our own server (no cookies, no third-party trackers, which also keeps the privacy notice true). Show them on an operator "Growth" page.
3. **WhatsApp click-to-chat** on the landing page and in the "I have paid" step, with the order reference prefilled in the message.
4. **Payment confirmation alerts.** Email or push the operator when an order reaches "I have paid", so confirmations happen within the hour. Later, automatic matching of mobile-money messages.
5. **Referral codes** and a "share Viro" button in the app after a successful fix.
6. **Lifecycle emails** from reach.viro3.online: day 0 welcome with how to pay, day 3 "run your first Fix my PC", day 30 Viro Move reminder, day 330 renewal. (The weekly care email already exists.)

## 9. Honesty rules for all marketing

Say Windows 10 and 11 only. Never claim a speed-up without the app's own measured before/after. The certificate states what was measured and when; do not describe it as a warranty. Do not use the "sample data" screenshots as if they were a customer. Prices shown must match the live plans.
