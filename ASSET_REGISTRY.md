## Quality status (honest)

The procedural Hunter and rifle are a **deliberate interim**: they are original, rights-clean, and not cubes or capsules, but they are not yet at the "premium" bar in the directive. They are built so a licensed model can replace them without touching gameplay code:

* **Hunter replacement slot** — `HunterBody` exposes the bone transforms (`Pelvis`, `Spine`, `Chest`, `Head`, arms, legs) that `HunterAnimator`, the hitboxes and the ragdoll use. A rigged humanoid (e.g. a Mixamo character, © Adobe, royalty-free for games) can be mapped onto the same bones.
* **Rifle replacement slot** — `RifleViewModel` only needs a `Muzzle` transform and a magazine transform for the reload choreography.

Candidate sources to evaluate (each must be added to this table with license proof before import): Mixamo characters/animations; Poly Haven (CC0) textures/HDRIs; Kenney (CC0) — rejected for the rifle/Hunter because its style is toy-like.
